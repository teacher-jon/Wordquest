📚 Syntax Sorcery Guide

Complete reference for spells and patterns

🔮 Spell Types

🔥
Projectile - Fire magical projectiles at enemies
Tier 1: Magic Bolt (10 Focus) Tier 2: Enhanced Bolt (15 Focus) Tier 3: Power Blast (20 Focus) Tier 4: Meteor Strike (25 Focus)
💚
Heal - Restore your health points
Tier 1: Minor Heal (10 Focus, +20 HP) Tier 2: Heal (15 Focus, +40 HP) Tier 3: Greater Heal (20 Focus, +60 HP) Tier 4: Full Restore (25 Focus, +100 HP)
⛏️
Break - Destroy tiles and obstacles
Tier 1: Crack (10 Focus, 1 tile) Tier 2: Shatter (15 Focus, 3x3 area) Tier 3: Demolish (20 Focus, 5x5 area) Tier 4: Earthquake (25 Focus, 7x7 area)
⚡
Dash - Teleport in the direction you're facing
Tier 1: Blink (10 Focus, 3 tiles) Tier 2: Dash (15 Focus, 5 tiles) Tier 3: Warp (20 Focus, 7 tiles) Tier 4: Teleport (25 Focus, 10 tiles)
🧊
Control - Freeze or slow enemies
Tier 1: Slow (10 Focus, 3s) Tier 2: Freeze (15 Focus, 5s) Tier 3: Stun (20 Focus, 7s) Tier 4: Petrify (25 Focus, 10s)

📝 Sentence Patterns

Pattern 1: Subject-Verb
Example: "Dragon flies" (Noun + Verb)
Base Power: 2 words
Pattern 2: Subject-Verb-Object
Example: "Wizard casts spell" (Noun + Verb + Noun)
Base Power: 3 words
Pattern 3: Adjective-Subject-Verb
Example: "Ancient dragon soars" (Adj + Noun + Verb)
Base Power: 3 words
Pattern 4: Subject-Verb-Adverb
Example: "Hero fights bravely" (Noun + Verb + Adv)
Base Power: 3 words

✨ Magic Power Tiers

Tier 0 (0-9 points): Too Weak - Cannot cast spells
Tier 1 (10-19 points): Basic - Unlocks basic spell effects
Tier 2 (20-29 points): Enhanced - Stronger spell effects
Tier 3 (30-39 points): Powerful - Very strong effects
Tier 4 (40+ points): Legendary - Maximum power!
💡 Tip: Use stronger words and longer sentences to increase magic power!
